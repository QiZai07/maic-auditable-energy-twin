AI-ASSISTED AUDITABLE DIGITAL TWIN FOR BUILDING ENERGY DECISIONS
TEAM ENERGEN AI · PROJECT IRENE
MAIC NEXUS CHALLENGE 2026 · T1 — AI FOR CLEAN ENERGY

Buildings often have meters, equipment schedules, drawings and utility records but still lack a defensible way to decide what to change, how much it may save and whether an investment is justified. Irene turns client-owned files into an auditable decision chain covering energy, cost, carbon, efficiency, photovoltaic generation and future storage. A human confirmation gate prevents unreviewed values from entering the calculation layer, while deterministic methods keep every result reproducible and traceable.

The public demonstration uses an anonymized Ningbo reference case. Approved aggregate evidence includes 345,676.69 kWh/year of electricity, a 29.01% HVAC share, a gross floor area of 6,231.26 m², 106.14 kWp of installed photovoltaic capacity and 126,233.50 kWh of annual PV generation. Original institutional meter rows, private weather records, drawings, donor records and client uploads are excluded from the public repository. Checked-in row-level load, weather, PV and comfort profiles are deterministic synthetic demonstration records constrained to the approved aggregate results.

Irene provides eight connected modules: project overview, monthly evidence, hourly evidence, efficiency and ROI, PV and future storage, digital-twin evidence, analysis agent, and client data onboarding. CSV, Excel, PDF, Word, image, DXF, IFC and DWG inputs can enter a session-only review workflow. Confirmed tables can be consolidated into an energy baseline and a privacy-safe delivery pack containing mappings, quality records, evidence lineage and an audit log without redistributing raw client uploads.

For model-assisted questions, Irene sends only the user’s question and summaries returned by deterministic project tools; raw files and unconfirmed rows are not attached. Optional PDF/image recognition is a per-file exception that requires consent and uses `store: false`. ChatGPT supplies general-purpose language reasoning, while Irene adds approved project evidence, evidence classes, reproducible calculations, approval gates, offline fallback and an auditable delivery pack.

The combined screening package—HVAC optimisation, operating-hour control, LED retrofit and plug-load management—estimates P10/P50/P90 savings of 31,186.27, 41,581.69 and 51,977.11 kWh/year under stated engineering assumptions. At the Ningbo reference tariff of CNY 0.538/kWh, the P50 case corresponds to CNY 22,370.95/year and a 2.77-year simple payback. The 30.77 tCO₂e/year figure is a parameterized Malaysia carbon scenario, not a Malaysian field result. No battery is currently installed; a separate 300 kWh/120 kW sandbox evaluates future loss-aware dispatch.

The first paid product is a file-based energy diagnosis with a confirmed baseline, auditable report and action shortlist for commercial buildings, campuses, industrial parks and energy service companies. Revenue can combine a one-off diagnosis fee, annual subscription, and implementation plus measurement-and-verification services. The pilot pathway is file audit, temporary metering, calibration, savings verification and multi-site expansion.

Irene is validated on a Ningbo reference case and designed for configurable deployment in Malaysia, pending local pilot validation with authorized client data.
